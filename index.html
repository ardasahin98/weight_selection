    Weight elicitation for the cyclic resistance logic tree

    This exercise collects your judgment on the relative weights of the branches in the logic tree for cyclic resistance assessment. Your responses, together with those of the other panel members, will inform the weighting guidance in the final report.

    What you are being asked

    You will work through a number of weight sets. In each, you distribute weight across the available branches so that the weights sum to 1.000. A weight of 0 is a legitimate answer — it means you would not use that branch at all under those conditions.

    Structure of the logic tree

    Stage 1
    Fines Content Classification
    Split on P[FC < FCth]. Fixed for this exercise: FCth is taken as a single value. Not elicited.
    Stage 2
    Susceptibility (S‑CBT models)
    Median and ±1σ model results. Elicited in Section A.
    Stage 3
    Assessment Type
    P[S] vs 1−P[S]. Fixed by the S‑CBT model output. Not elicited.
    Stage 4
    Cyclic Resistance
    Assessment types per case. Elicited in Sections B–D.
